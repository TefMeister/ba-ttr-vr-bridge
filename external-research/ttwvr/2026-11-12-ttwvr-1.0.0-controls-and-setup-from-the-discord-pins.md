# TTWVR 1.0.0: setup and controls, from the author's pinned Discord messages (passed on by Tefa, 2026-11-12)

Source: KUPOkinz's pinned messages in the TTWVR thread on the Flat2VR Discord, copied by Tefa into a Developer PC session
on 2026-11-12. Private notes: this repo is private; the public control profile carries only our own short summary.
`[reported]` (the author's own words; not each one tried by us yet).

## Virtual Desktop vs ReShade's OpenXR layer

If the game started under VDXR falls back to SteamVR and full body breaks, the author's fix is to switch off ReShade's
32-bit OpenXR layer (`C:\ProgramData\ReShade\ReShade32_XR.json` under
`HKLM\SOFTWARE\WOW6432Node\Khronos\OpenXR\1\ApiLayers\Implicit`, value set to 1 = disabled; needs an administrator
window). Checked on the Developer PC 2026-11-12: no ReShade folder and no 32-bit machine-wide layers at all, so it does
not apply here `[measured]`.

## Setup

Stand straight up and open Virtual Desktop (or SteamVR on the Frame): this sets the height for the mod's body tracking.
Turn ON "Forward tracking data" in the Virtual Desktop app on the Quest (Streaming settings), then start the game under
VDXR or SteamVR. In game, sit or stand as you like, hold the "mummy pose" (both arms straight out in front) and hold BOTH
triggers: calibrates the height and the floor (repeatable any time).

## Controls (the author's text, verbatim)

[BASIC CONTROLS]
Movement = Move the Left Stick in any direction.
Turning = Move the Right Stick left or right.
Crouch/Sneak = Move the Right Stick down.
Jump = Move the Right Stick up.
Activate = A Button or X Button (depending on which hand is pointing).
VATS = B Button.
Favorite Menu = Click the Right Stick, move the Right Stick in a direction, then release.
Wait = Click the Left Stick.
Pause = Y Button.
Grab Items = Left Grip or Right Grip.
Scroll Loot Menu = Move the Right Stick while looting.

[AMBIDEX FIREARM CONTROLS]
Grip Weapon = Left Grip or Right Grip (draw from either shoulder).
Attack = Left Trigger or Right Trigger.
Interact = Use the Left Trigger or Right Trigger on bolts, slides, etc.
Drop Ammo = A Button or X Button.
Save Ammo = Hold Left Grip or Right Grip + A Button or X Button.
Weapon Smoothing = Hold Left Trigger or Right Trigger.
Scope Change = Left Trigger or Right Trigger.
Bolt Lock = Pull the slide/bolt back, then press A Button or X Button.
Pump Actions = Hold A or X, then pump the action with Right or Left Grip.
Open/Close Gates = Press A Button or X Button, then flick the wrist.

[AMBIDEX MELEE CONTROLS]
Grip Weapon = Left Grip or Right Grip (draw from either shoulder).
Attack = Swing the weapon naturally with force.
Heavy Attack = Hold Left Trigger or Right Trigger.
Special Attack = Move in a direction, then perform a Heavy Attack.
Block = Hold Left Trigger or Right Trigger.
Punch = Hold Left Grip or Right Grip while your hand is empty.

[AMBIDEX THROWABLE CONTROLS]
Grip Weapon = Left Grip or Right Grip (draw from either shoulder).
Prime Explosives = Pull with the other hand using Left Grip or Right Grip.
Throw = Release Left Grip or Right Grip.

[WRIST-BOY CONTROLS]
Turn ON/OFF = Right Trigger while your hand is near the Wrist-Boy.
Swap Categories = Hold Right Trigger + Left Grip or Right Grip.
Swap Pages = Left Grip or Right Grip.
Various Menu Controls = Labeled On-Screen.
Open/Close Paused Pip-Boy = Left Trigger while your hand is near the Wrist-Boy.
Flashlight = Hold Left Trigger while your hand is near the Wrist-Boy.

[ADVANCED CONTROLS]
Use Live-Stim = Grip from the neck area, then press Right Trigger or Left Trigger to activate. Stab into body.
Open Console Window = Click and hold the Left Stick.
Offhand Bashing = Perform a punch with your empty hand while holding a weapon.
Holsters = Grab from either hip or either shoulder using Left Grip or Right Grip.
Backpack Loot = Release items over either shoulder.
Adjust Ammo Types = Hold Left Trigger or Right Trigger on the "Ammo Pouch" for 1 second.
Adjust Spare Pouch Position = Hold Left Grip or Right Grip for 3 seconds, then move and release.
Gravity Gloves = Point at an item, then press and hold Left Grip or Right Grip while flicking back.
Voice = Hold A Button while in conversations, speak and then release.
Gun Bash = Swing Weapon at enemy.
